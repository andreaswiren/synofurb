synofurb: UX layout and flow mockups

Wireframe-level layouts, flows and copy for the desktop app and the NAS management plane. Static HTML, open from disk. Each mockup has a screen switcher bar at the top; buttons inside the mockups navigate too. All data is sample data; every model is Untested except DS923+. Reference unit: DS918+ at 192.168.1.40. Revised after the independent UX review: desktop Concept A and mgmt-a only.
