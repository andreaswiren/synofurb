Withdrawn: rejected by the owner

synofurb design review

Three layouts and three visual directions for the desktop app, and two layouts for the web interface that runs on the NAS. The owner rejected all of them: too much text, GPUI components not used properly, no Help or About, wrong fonts and icons, unthemed scrollbars, no logo. The critics had only returned "approve with conditions", and nobody re-reviewed the revisions, so none of this was approved. It is kept as an archive. The replacement is being built directly in the real app, under the new rules in design/BRIEF-v2.md, and the critics will review screenshots of the real app.

What the critics said

Both reviews were written before the revisions and are kept unedited. The UX critic found 13 blocking problems, mostly screens that promised recovery the product cannot give. The UI critic found 6, among them that the enabled destructive button had never been drawn and that nothing had been rendered at the 960 by 640 minimum.

UX critic reviewUI critic review

  • UX: Concept A and mgmt-A approved with conditions. The conditions are met in the revised files; a re-review is the real test.
  • UI: Direction 1 approved with conditions; Directions 2 and 3 rejected as a base. After revision all 472 colour pairs pass; the lowest text ratio is 4.78:1.
  • These are advisory reviews. The formal gate verdicts are separate and still missing.

What you are not seeing yet

  • A signed-off design. The GPUI app already exists and follows Concept A and Direction 1 so you can judge the real thing, but its look will change if you choose differently.
  • Any usability testing. The test plan in the UX recommendation starts with the data-loss confirmation, because that screen carries the most risk.
  • Screen-reader support in the native app. GPUI Kit has accessibility support, but it has not been tested with a real screen reader. The mockups assume a documented command-line path as the fallback.
  • Protanopia, tritanopia and Windows high-contrast checks. All 66 text and background pairs pass WCAG AA in both themes as computed; the lowest ratio is 4.63:1.

How approval works

Tell me your three choices and any changes. After that, two separate reviewers (a UX critic and a UI critic) must each approve the design before any release build. Their approval is tied to a hash of the design files, so changing a design invalidates it until they review again.

Today the gate fails on purpose: no approvals exist yet.
